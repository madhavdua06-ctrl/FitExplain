# Research: Propra vs. Buildium — Verified Market Data

**Purpose:** Sourced, dated backing material for the `propra-vs-buildium-battlecard.md` sales tool. Every claim below is either sourced (with link + access date) or explicitly marked unverified. This doc is internal reference material, not sales copy — the battle card distills it.

**Compiled:** 2026-08-04

---

## 1. Company snapshots

### Buildium
- Property management software: accounting, leasing, maintenance, communications, tenant screening, reporting. ([buildium.com/pricing](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/), accessed 2026-08-04)
- Acquired by **RealPage** for **$580M** in a deal announced Nov 6, 2019, closed Dec 18, 2019 — Buildium is now a RealPage-owned product line, not an independent company. ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/national/news/property-management/realpage-acquires-buildium-for-580m-furthers-consolidation-in-property-management-101779), [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/realpage-buys-buildium-for-580-million/), accessed 2026-08-04)
- At acquisition (2019): ~17,000 property owners, ~2M units under management. This is a 2019 figure and almost certainly higher today — **no current verified unit/customer count found**; do not quote the 17K/2M figures as current in the battle card without an updated source.
- Target segment per RealPage's own rationale: small and midsized owners of multifamily/single-family properties, associations, smaller commercial real estate owners. (Bisnow, accessed 2026-08-04)

### Propra
- Canadian, accounting-first property management platform. Tagline: **"Control, Trust, Scale"** / "Stay in control as you grow." ([propra.tech](https://propra.tech/), accessed 2026-08-04)
- Target market (their own positioning): Canadian property management companies — single-family, multi-family, condo, assisted living, commercial — from boutique operators to large enterprises; both owner-operators and third-party property managers. (propra.tech, accessed 2026-08-04)
- Founded 2021, HQ Calgary, AB. Founder/CEO: Al-Karim Khimji. ([Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/propra), accessed 2026-08-04)
- Crunchbase lists **$70K raised** (pre-seed-stage backers: NAR REACH, Calgary Fintech Award, Creative Destruction Lab, Plug and Play Alberta, Alberta Accelerator by 500, and others) and only 2 tracked employee profiles. **Caveat:** Crunchbase's "employee profiles" count is frequently stale/incomplete for small private companies and should not be read as an accurate current headcount — flag internally rather than repeat externally. (Crunchbase, accessed 2026-08-04)
- Self-reported performance metrics (propra.tech homepage, unverified by a third party — treat as company-stated, not independently audited): **$70M+ payments processed, 25,000+ units managed, 99.99% uptime, 24/7 support**, typical onboarding in **2–3 weeks**. (propra.tech, accessed 2026-08-04)

**Read-through:** This is a small, young Canadian challenger (Propra) going up against a RealPage-owned incumbent (Buildium) with a much larger installed base. That size gap cuts both ways — see Section 5.

## 2. Pricing — Buildium (verified, first-party)

Source: [buildium.com/pricing](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/), accessed 2026-08-04. Note: some third-party listing sites (SoftwareAdvice, itqlick, etc.) quote a slightly different base of $58/mo for Essential — the live pricing page is the authoritative figure and is used below.

| Tier | Base price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | $62/mo | Basic accounting, maintenance, leasing; basic communications; standard/bulk reporting |
| **Growth** ("Most Popular") | $192/mo | Everything in Essential + customization, reporting automation, enhanced tenant screening, lower-cost eSignatures/EFT, AI communications, business analytics |
| **Premium** | $400/mo | Everything in Growth + Open API, free inspections, free eSignatures, first-year free incoming EFT, full AI/automation suite, "up to $500 of free leads" |

Per-unit pricing above included thresholds is **not publicly disclosed** — Buildium requires a sales call for exact quotes at scale. (buildium.com/pricing, accessed 2026-08-04)

(All figures: buildium.com/pricing, accessed 2026-08-04)

**Reconciling the original note** ("the lower tier plan costs an extra 100 to set up the account — hidden fees"): the closest verified match is the **Essential-tier bank account setup fee of $99** — i.e., on Buildium's cheapest plan, a customer pays ~$100 just to set up the bank account needed to collect rent/process payments, a cost that's waived (100 free) on Premium. This is a real, sourced, first-party number and should be the figure used in the battle card — not a rounded "$100" guess. (There's a second, separate $99 setup fee for property inspections on Essential — worth knowing this exists too, in case a prospect brings up inspections specifically.)

## 4. Feature/positioning comparison

| Area | Buildium | Propra |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting | Full accounting module, described by third parties as solid core/trust accounting | Built "around financials from day one" — accounting wired directly to lease, maintenance, and payment events rather than bolted on (propra.tech, accessed 2026-08-04) |
| Geography/compliance | US-centric; RealPage/US real estate ecosystem | Canadian-built: GST/PST/HST workflows and Canadian payment processing handled as core platform logic (propra.tech, accessed 2026-08-04) |
| Maintenance | Users report it as a weak point — see Section 5 | Maintenance coordination, mobile inspections (move-in/move-out/recurring) listed as core features (propra.tech, accessed 2026-08-04); note DoorLoop's review criticizes Propra's maintenance flow as email-based rather than portal-based — flag as a real product gap to know about, not to hide from reps |
| Fee structure | Tiered with per-transaction add-ons (see Section 2) that scale down as you pay more — the incentive structure nudges customers to a pricier tier to avoid fees | Not publicly documented; needs internal confirmation (Section 3) |
| AI/automation | AI communications (Growth+), full AI suite (Premium) | AI tools for bill capture, reconciliation, and workflow automation, per propra.tech |
| Onboarding | Not publicly timed; Essential-tier bank setup carries the $99 fee noted above | Self-reported "2–3 weeks" typical onboarding (propra.tech, unverified by third party) |

## 5. Review sentiment (third-party, aggregated)

### Buildium
- **Capterra:** ~4.5/5 stars, 2,200+ reviews as of March 2026. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/47428/Buildium-Property-Management-Software/reviews/), accessed 2026-08-04)
- **Recurring praise:** core/trust accounting, clean owner & tenant statements, reliable bank reconciliation, welcoming interface.
- **Recurring complaints:**
  - Maintenance feature described by some reviewers as weak enough that they paid for a separate third-party tool to cover the gap.
  - Customer support inconsistency — some reviewers describe long ticket delays and undertrained reps; others report good phone support. Sentiment is mixed, not uniformly bad — **present this as "support quality varies by reviewer," not "support is bad."**
  - Pricing-transparency complaints despite Buildium's own marketing claiming "no hidden fees" — including reports of a bank account freeze during underwriting review and abrupt integration/pricing changes cited by individual reviewers. These are anecdotal (single-reviewer) reports, not audited facts — cite as "reviewers report," not as established fact.
  - Enhanced Tenant Screening reportedly does not include criminal history checks, per reviewer feedback.
- Sources: search aggregation of G2 ([g2.com/products/buildium/reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/buildium/reviews)) and Capterra review pages, accessed 2026-08-04. G2's page itself returned a 403 to direct fetch; the summary above is from search-indexed review content, so treat specific quotes as paraphrased, not verbatim.

### Propra
- **G2:** Not enough reviews for G2 to generate a rating (effectively 0 reviews indexed as of 2026-08-04).
- **Capterra:** no distinct star rating surfaced in research; Propra has a listing but limited independent review volume.
- **Read-through:** Propra doesn't yet have a public review base to point to. The battle card should lean on product differentiation and named reference customers (if the user can supply any) rather than claiming review-site superiority — that claim isn't supportable right now.

## 6. Landmines Buildium could plant against Propra (know these before the call)

Fair to the sales team to know both directions:
- **Company size/stability:** Propra is a small, young company (founded 2021, ~2 tracked employees per Crunchbase, ~$70K raised) vs. a RealPage-backed incumbent with a large installed base. A prospect or competing rep may raise "will they still be around / can they support us at scale."
- **Maintenance workflow gap:** at least one competitor review (DoorLoop) describes Propra's maintenance requests as email-driven rather than in-portal — worth confirming internally whether this is still accurate before a rep gets caught flat-footed.
- **Pricing opacity:** Propra doesn't publish pricing either, so "Buildium hides fees" lands better if Propra's own pricing/setup costs are demonstrably simpler once disclosed in a demo — the card should not imply Propra is fully transparent unless that's actually true.
