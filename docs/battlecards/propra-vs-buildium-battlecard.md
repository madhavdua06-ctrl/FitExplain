# Battle Card: Propra vs. Buildium

Internal sales enablement — not for external distribution. Backing data and sources: `propra-vs-buildium-research.md` in this folder. Last updated 2026-08-04.

## 30-second positioning

Buildium is a US-built, RealPage-owned property management platform with a large installed base and a tiered-pricing model where the cheapest plan carries the most add-on fees. Propra is a Canadian, accounting-native platform built for teams that want their books wired directly to leases, maintenance, and payments — with Canadian tax/compliance (GST/PST/HST) handled natively rather than bolted on.

**Lead with:** accounting-first architecture + Canadian compliance fit. **Back it up with:** Buildium's Essential-tier fee stack when pricing comes up.

## Why we win

1. **Accounting isn't an add-on.** Buildium's accounting is a module inside a broader suite; Propra's platform is architected around the ledger — lease, maintenance, and payment events post directly to the books. For portfolios where clean, real-time financials are the #1 pain point, this is a structural advantage, not a feature checkbox.
2. **Built for Canada, not adapted for it.** GST/PST/HST handling and Canadian payment rails are core platform logic, not a regional patch. For a Canadian prospect currently on a US-built tool, this removes a category of workaround entirely.
3. **Pricing transparency as a sales conversation, not a landmine.** Buildium's cheapest plan (Essential, $62/mo) is also the one with the most à-la-carte fees — see "landmine" below. Propra should aim to win the "what will I actually pay" conversation, but only quote Propra's own numbers once they're internally confirmed (see Open Items).

## Landmine: the Essential-tier fee stack

This is the verified version of the "hidden fees" note that kicked off this card. Use it, but state it precisely — don't round up or exaggerate:

> "On Buildium's entry-level Essential plan, setting up the bank account you need to actually collect rent costs an extra **$99** — that's on top of the $62/month base. Property inspections carry a separate $99 setup fee too. Move up to Premium and both are waived — but now you're at $400/month. Essential customers also pay per-eSignature ($5/doc) and higher EFT fees ($2.35 vs. as low as $0.60 on Premium). The cheapest sticker price isn't the cheapest real cost."

**Do not say** "Buildium hides fees" as an accusation — their fee schedule is publicly posted on buildium.com/pricing. The honest framing is: *the fees are disclosed, but they're structured so the advertised low price doesn't reflect what most customers actually pay.* That's accurate and still lands.

## Objection handling

**"Buildium has way more reviews / is more established."**
True — Buildium has ~2,200+ Capterra reviews at ~4.5 stars and is RealPage-owned. Don't contest that. Redirect: "That scale comes from a US-built platform serving a US-heavy customer base. The question isn't who has more reviews, it's whether a US-general tool or a Canadian-native one fits your compliance and accounting workflow better." Then go to the accounting/GST-HST differentiator.

**"Isn't Propra a really small company? What if you don't last?"**
Don't deflect — this is a fair question given Propra is a young, small team. Address it directly with whatever concrete stability signals the company can offer (funding runway, customer retention, uptime track record — Propra's own site claims 99.99% uptime, $70M+ processed, 25K+ units managed; confirm these are current before citing) rather than dodging the size question.

**"Buildium says they have zero hidden fees."**
That's their marketing claim, not the full fee schedule. Point to the specific, sourced numbers above (setup fees, per-document eSignature costs, EFT tiering) — these are pulled directly from Buildium's own public pricing page, not third-party rumor.

**"What does Propra cost?"**
Do not improvise a number. Third-party listings suggest a per-unit model (~$3–$20/unit/month depending on tier, free under 30 units) but this is **not confirmed by Propra directly** — get the current, approved pricing from sales leadership before quoting a prospect.

## Discovery questions to steer toward our strengths

- "How much manual reconciliation work does your team do between your PM tool and your books today?" (surfaces the accounting-bolt-on pain Buildium users report)
- "Are you tracking GST/PST/HST manually or through a workaround right now?" (Canadian-fit wedge)
- "Have you priced out what your setup and per-transaction fees actually add up to on your current/prospective plan?" (opens the Essential-tier fee conversation without leading with an accusation)
- "What does your maintenance request workflow look like today?" (use carefully — see Open Items; don't over-promise on Propra's own maintenance UX until confirmed)

## Traps to avoid

- Don't quote Propra pricing numbers — none are internally confirmed as of this card's last update.
- Don't claim Buildium "hides" fees — they're publicly posted; the honest angle is that the advertised price is misleading about real cost, not that fees are concealed.
- Don't claim superior reviews/ratings — Propra doesn't have a meaningful public review base yet; don't invite a review-site comparison.
- Don't over-claim on maintenance UX — at least one third-party review flags Propra's maintenance flow as email-based rather than in-portal; confirm current state before contradicting a prospect who's done their homework.
- 2019 Buildium customer/unit counts (17K owners, 2M units) are stale — don't cite them as current.
